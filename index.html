Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending Boba Bash Greater Noida.

What is Boba Bash? +
Boba Bash is an in-person, student-run hackathon and creative coding jam. Teens come together to build projects (websites, games, apps, hardware, design prototypes), learn from mentors, eat great food, and enjoy fresh bubble tea.
Who can participate? +
Any middle school or high school student (ages ~13–18) in Greater Noida, Noida, Ghaziabad, and across NCR is welcome!
I have never coded before. Can I still attend? +
YES, 100%! Boba Bash is explicitly built to be beginner-friendly. Right after our opening ceremony, we host a dedicated beginner Web Dev Workshop where you'll learn how to build and style a website from scratch with friendly mentor guidance!
How do the RFID-enabled ID cards work? +
Every participant receives an RFID-enabled ID badge at check-in. During the project shipping session, our team will program your badge with your live website link at the RFID station. During the community showcase, attendees can simply tap your card with their phones to instantly open your website!
How much does it cost? +
It is completely free (₹0)! Thanks to our sponsors and community partners, your admission, meals, boba drinks, stickers, and swag are fully covered.
What should I bring? +
Bring your laptop, charger, student ID, a water bottle, and a curious mindset! If you don't have a laptop, reach out to our team in advance and we'll try our best to work out a solution for you!
Are the boba tea, food, and merch really free? +
YES, 100%! Thanks to our generous sponsors and partners, freshly brewed bubble tea, hot lunch, snacks, sticker packs, and custom event merchandise are completely free for all registered participants.
